A few useful answers

Before you make plans.

What time is check-in?

The sample hotel policy is check-in from 2:00 PM and checkout by noon. Early arrival and late departure are requests, not guarantees.

Is breakfast included?

Deluxe rooms and suites include the breakfast stated on the room card. Standard Room breakfast is optional unless you choose a package.

Can I work from my room?

The concept includes a work desk and Wi-Fi in every room. Connection speeds are not guaranteed; ask about your meeting needs.
