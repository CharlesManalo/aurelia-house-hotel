Iloilo City · Boutique city hotel

A place with a point of view.

An imagined boutique address, shaped around the way people really travel: sometimes for work, sometimes for a change of scene, always for a good night’s rest.

Background

Small scale. Thoughtful stays.

Aurelia House is a fictional, independently styled hotel concept in Iloilo City. Its compact scale lets the guest experience focus on practical comfort, thoughtful spaces, and a personal welcome.

Target market

Who we welcome.

Business travelers, couples, and families looking for a comfortable city stay with space to work and time to explore.

Mission

What guides us.

To make city travel feel simpler through comfortable rooms, clear information, and attentive service.

Vision

Where we hope to go.

To become a welcoming base that helps visitors appreciate Iloilo’s neighborhoods, heritage, and local food.
